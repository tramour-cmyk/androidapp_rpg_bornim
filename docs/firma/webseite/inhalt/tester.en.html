Testers wanted

Be among the first to play the Chronicles of Bornim, and help us make them great.

What it is about

On the night you sleep at the inn of Bornim, the goblins of the Bloodfang Tribe raid the temple. They carry off Sister Lyra and steal the Sun Amulet, which has shielded the village from the darkness for a hundred years. In the morning you are sent out: through the Whisperwood, to the Bloodfang Cave, all the way to Captain Grak. What you find on him is only the start of a story told over seven chapters.

What makes it different

Dark, grown-up fantasy with no sugar-coating: wolves that hunt in packs, goblins that lay ambushes, a forest where you had better stay on the path. Light comes from torches and fires, ink lines draw the world like an old chronicle, and you hear what is there: wind, fire, footsteps, the village at work. You build your hero from four classes (Fighter, Wizard, Rogue, Cleric) and five races; the rules are based on the role-playing rule set SRD 5.1, made simpler and faster. Battles are fought turn by turn, and every turn is a real choice.

And it stays honest: no ads, no loot boxes, no pay-to-skip timers. You may grind, but you never have to.

What's in it for you

  • You play Chapter 1 before it comes out.
  • Your feedback goes straight to the people building the game; we read every message and build in what makes the game better.
  • You see a game take shape, and you play a part in it.

What you do

You install the game through Google Play and play whenever you like. What breaks, what bothers you, what you enjoy: you tell us, as briefly or as fully as you like. It is an early build: bugs and gaps are part of it, and they are exactly what we are looking for.

What you need

  • an Android phone
  • a Google account
  • 14 consecutive days in which you stay signed up as a tester and play now and then

How the test works

Why the address of your Google account? Before release, only accounts we have added in advance can join a closed test on Google Play. Any other address won't get you in.

  1. We add you and send you an email with a link.
  2. Open the link while signed in with the Google account whose address you sent us (if you have several, please check). It leads to a Google Play page, where you agree to join the test.
  3. Then install the Chronicles from the Play Store as usual. It can take a few hours before the game shows up for you.
  4. Updates usually arrive automatically.
  5. Stay signed up as a tester for 14 consecutive days (Google requires this before we may release the game) and play now and then. If you leave and sign up again, the count starts over.
  6. You can leave at any time, through the Google Play page from step 2 or by emailing us.
