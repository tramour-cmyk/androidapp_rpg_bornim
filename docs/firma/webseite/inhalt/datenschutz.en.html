3. The app “Chronicles of Bornim”

Data on your device

The app keeps saved games, settings and a crash log on your device only. They stay in the app’s private storage area, which other apps cannot read. We never receive them. You delete saved games in the app; uninstalling removes everything. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back these data up to your Google account; Google does this under your settings, and we have no access to it.

Permissions

The app asks for no access to the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or your files. Its only permission lets it adjust audio settings for music and sound in the game.

Google Play

If you get the app from Google Play, Google processes data for this as an independent controller under the Google Privacy Policy. From Google we receive only aggregate figures (such as installs and ratings) and crash and performance reports that Google collects from devices whose users allowed it. We cannot identify individuals from them.

Taking part in tests

People who test the app before release give us the email address of their Google account, so that we can add them as testers in Google Play, and send us feedback. We pass the address to Google for this; Google may process it outside the EU and relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Without the address you cannot take part. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (your participation). We delete the address when the test ends or you leave. If you receive an allowance for testing, we keep the receipt as long as tax law requires (up to ten years, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
